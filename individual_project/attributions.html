Attributions

Sources and acknowledgements for this educational project.

Primary source: Physics I Laboratory Guide

The experimental topics, laboratory activities, and instructional content presented on this website are based on the Physics I Laboratory Guide used in the Physics program at Universidad Autónoma Gabriel René Moreno (UAGRM), Santa Cruz, Bolivia.

This website reorganizes and presents educational material from that guide in a responsive digital format to support students in reviewing laboratory experiments, their objectives, required materials, and procedures.

The original laboratory guide is the primary reference for the experiment content. Students should consult their official course materials and follow the instructions provided by their laboratory instructor.

Laboratory Experiments and Content

The experiments, procedures, and laboratory content presented on this website are based on the Physics I Laboratory Guide used in the Physics program at Universidad Autónoma Gabriel René Moreno (UAGRM), Santa Cruz, Bolivia.

The material has been organized into a digital format for educational purposes. The original laboratory guide is the source for the experimental content. Students should consult the official guide and follow their laboratory instructor's directions.
