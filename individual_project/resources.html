Physics I Laboratory Resources

Review the fundamental concepts of Physics I, prepare your laboratory equipment, record measurements, and analyze experimental results.

01

Measurement and Error Analysis

Understand how measurements and experimental errors affect your results.

  • Identify the physical quantity being measured.
  • Record measurements with the correct units.
  • Consider instrument precision and measurement uncertainty.
  • Repeat measurements when required by the procedure.
  • Calculate averages and analyze experimental errors.
02

Laboratory Safety

Follow safe practices when working with equipment, forces, and moving objects.

  • Follow the instructor's directions before starting an experiment.
  • Inspect equipment, supports, strings, and moving components.
  • Keep the work area clear of unnecessary objects.
  • Handle weights and mechanical equipment carefully.
  • Report damaged equipment or unsafe conditions.
03

Equipment and Measurements

Choose appropriate instruments for the physical quantities studied in Physics I.

  • Check the measuring range and resolution of each instrument.
  • Use a ruler or measuring tape to determine distances.
  • Use a stopwatch to measure time intervals.
  • Check masses, angles, and force measurements as required.
  • Record the units and relevant instrument precision.
04

Data Analysis and Results

Use experimental data to study motion, forces, and energy.

  • Organize measurements in tables with clear headings and units.
  • Calculate quantities such as velocity, acceleration, and force when applicable.
  • Compare experimental results with theoretical predictions.
  • Construct graphs with clearly labeled axes and units.
  • Explain possible sources of error in your conclusions.
