NP Membership

  • No membership fee.
  • Access to selected chamber events.
  • Networking opportunities.
  • Access to nonprofit resources.

Bronze Membership

  • Access to chamber events.
  • Business networking opportunities.
  • Member directory listing.
  • Access to selected training activities.

Silver Membership

  • All Bronze membership benefits.
  • Additional training opportunities.
  • Business promotion opportunities.
  • Priority access to selected events.

Gold Membership

  • All Silver membership benefits.
  • Enhanced business promotion.
  • Additional networking opportunities.
  • Priority access to chamber activities.
